## M01 — On Practice / 实践论
- Author: Mao Zedong.
- URL: https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-1/mswv1_16.htm
- Chinese original-language parallel: https://news.12371.cn/2017/08/29/ARTI1504001411770206.shtml — Mao Zedong, 实践论, July 1937 as headed; webpage publication field not verified (URL date alone is not proof). Opening production/class paragraphs and knowledge/practice passages read on 2026-11-03. Official-platform reproduction, not manuscript. Chinese wording ties the argument to historical production and social relations; no additional quotation retained.
- Date: July 1937 as attributed in Selected Works; webpage publication undated.
- Location read: opening production/class account; closing knowledge–practice cycle.
- Summary: Knowledge is socially situated and returns to transformative activity. Production and class struggle are substantive commitments, beyond business metaphors.
- Limits: Published English translation/transcription, not a manuscript. A philosophical political claim is not universal empirical proof.

## M02 — On Contradiction / 矛盾论
- Author: Mao Zedong.
- URL: https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-1/mswv1_17.htm
- Chinese original-language parallel: https://www.marxists.org/chinese/maozedong/marxist.org-chinese-mao-193708.htm — Mao Zedong, 矛盾论, August 1937 attribution; webpage undated. Headnote and section IV read on 2026-11-03. Its native Chinese contrasts principal relationships and aspects with firm statements followed by concrete historical cases. MIA selected-work transcription; headnote acknowledges revision, not a manuscript or oral recording.
- Date: August 1937 attribution; revised for Selected Works; webpage publication undated.
- Location read: sections I, III, IV, including changing alliances and principal aspects.
- Summary: Materialist dialectics separates the principal contradiction between relationships from the dominant aspect within one relationship. Historical conditions change political alliances.
- Limits: Author-revised published text, not untouched 1937 lecture notes. Not every priority or bottleneck is a philosophical contradiction.

## M03 — Talks at the Yenan Forum on Literature and Art / 在延安文艺座谈会上的讲话
- Author: Mao Zedong.
- URL: https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-3/mswv3_08.htm
- Date: May 2 and May 23, 1942 as headed; webpage publication undated.
- Location read: introduction on class/audience; conclusion on popularization and standards.
- Summary: Art is situated within revolutionary politics and a particular audience. Accessibility and artistic development are related, not identical.
- Limits: Edited selected-work text; does not establish private taste or prove political control improves artistic quality.

## M04 — On the Correct Handling of Contradictions Among the People / 关于正确处理人民内部矛盾的问题
- Author: Mao Zedong.
- URL: https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-5/mswv5_58.htm
- Date: speech February 27, 1957; revised publication June 19, 1957, per editorial headnote.
- Location read: headnote; section I on categories, persuasion and political leadership.
- Summary: The published argument distinguishes persuasion within the people from antagonism toward enemies. Those categories remain politically defined within socialist leadership.
- Limits: Publication-edited speech, not verbatim February delivery. The text's freedom claims cannot establish how campaigns actually treated dissent.

## M07 — Swimming / 水调歌头·游泳
- Author: Mao Zedong.
- URL: https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/poems/poems23.htm
- Chinese original-language parallel: https://cpc.people.com.cn/n1/2021/0807/c64387-32184920.html — Mao Zedong's whole poem reproduced at the opening of 展宏图 惊世界 by Fan Haotian; host publication August 7, 2021, 08:26, People's Daily page 05. Only the authored poem is used as primary expression evidence; the reporter's later celebratory claims are not Mao's speech or an engineering-effects assessment. This is a reproduction, not a manuscript.

- Location read: whole short poem.
- Summary: Bodily ease, classical reference and imagined engineering turn a river scene into a vision of remaking the landscape.
- Limits: Translated literary text; planned engineering is not evidence of completed or harmless engineering.

## M08 — Combat Liberalism / 反对自由主义
- Author: Mao Zedong.
- URL: https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-2/mswv2_03.htm
- Date: September 7, 1937 as headed; webpage publication undated.
- Location read: eleven types and conclusion on communist conduct.
- Summary: Organizational loyalty and ideological struggle outrank private accommodation. The polemic distinguishes personal quarrels from principled political disagreement.
- Limits: Party disciplinary argument; its liberalism is not a neutral definition of liberal political philosophy. Do not use it to police users' personal relationships.
