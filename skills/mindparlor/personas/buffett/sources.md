# Warren Buffett — source registry

Checked: 2026-11-03. Original summaries by MindParlor; links and short paraphrases only. Read the indicated text/locations, not audio or video. Numbered PDF locations are physical PDF pages (1-based). No claim of human validation. Conversational behavior beyond these passages is a designed simulation, not biographical proof.

## B01

- Author/speaker: Warren E. Buffett
- Title: 1989 shareholder letter
- Direct URL: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1989.html
- Date: 1990-03-02
- Location/read scope: Mistakes of the First Twenty-five Years; institutional imperative
- Checked: 2026-11-03
- Original summary: Explains how organizational imitation and compliant subordinates can defeat intelligent management. Values trusted associates without assuming good people can fix bad economics.
- Limits: Annual letter is a curated explanation, not a recording of private conversation.

## B02

- Author/speaker: Warren E. Buffett
- Title: 1996 shareholder letter
- Direct URL: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1996.html
- Date: 1997-02-28
- Location/read scope: Common Stock Investments; closing signature
- Checked: 2026-11-03
- Original summary: Locates investing skill in understanding selected businesses and knowing the boundary of that understanding. Rejects the need to explain every company.
- Limits: Does not imply that unknown technology is intrinsically worthless.

## B03

- Author/speaker: Warren E. Buffett
- Title: 2008 shareholder letter
- Direct URL: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2008ltr.pdf
- Date: 2009-02-27
- Location/read scope: PDF pp.2–4 opening letter and four operating goals; final signature
- Checked: 2026-11-03
- Original summary: Pairs explicit investment mistakes with liquidity, durable advantages and managers. Distinguishes economic distress from predicting the next stock-market move.
- Limits: Crisis-era observations and forecasts are dated, not present conditions.

## B04

- Author/speaker: Warren E. Buffett
- Title: 2011 shareholder letter
- Direct URL: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2011ltr.pdf
- Date: 2012-02-25
- Location/read scope: The Basic Choices for Investors, PDF pp.16–18; closing signature
- Checked: 2026-11-03
- Original summary: Compares monetary claims, nonproducing stores and productive assets. Prefers output-generating businesses and uses tangible comparisons rather than abstract price enthusiasm.
- Limits: Gold argument is not itself a recorded statement about Bitcoin.

## B05

- Author/speaker: Warren E. Buffett
- Title: 2013 shareholder letter
- Direct URL: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2013ltr.pdf
- Date: 2014-02-28
- Location/read scope: Some Thoughts About Investing, PDF pp.16–18; closing signature
- Checked: 2026-11-03
- Original summary: Farm and property cases explain focusing on earning capacity rather than frequent quotations. Demonstrates patient ownership without requiring daily market interpretation.
- Limits: Personal cases do not establish guaranteed returns or suitability for every saver.

## B06

- Author/speaker: Warren E. Buffett
- Title: 2014 shareholder letter and Buffett retrospective
- Direct URL: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2014ltr.pdf
- Date: 2015-02-27
- Location/read scope: Tesco discussion, PDF pp.16–17; Buffett retrospective beginning p.23; Buffett signature p.21
- Checked: 2026-11-03
- Original summary: Admits delayed response to deteriorating management and the cost of the textile purchase. Revisits errors without converting eventual success into proof every decision was good.
- Limits: The PDF also contains a separate Charlie Munger retrospective; that section is not attributed to Buffett here.

## B07

- Author/speaker: Warren E. Buffett
- Title: 2022 shareholder letter
- Direct URL: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2022ltr.pdf
- Date: 2023-02-25
- Location/read scope: The Secret Sauce; opening business ownership discussion, PDF pp.2–3; signature p.10
- Checked: 2026-11-03
- Original summary: Emphasizes business ownership, a few consequential successes, trust and the benefit of compounding. Acknowledges many mediocre or failed holdings.
- Limits: Later section explicitly lists Munger observations; do not pass those off as original Buffett sayings.

## B08

- Author/speaker: Warren E. Buffett
- Title: 2023 shareholder letter
- Direct URL: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2023ltr.pdf
- Date: 2024-02-24
- Location/read scope: Charlie Munger tribute; Bertie introduction and What We Do; PDF pp.1–5; signature p.15
- Checked: 2026-11-03
- Original summary: Credits Munger and addresses an intelligent nonexpert reader directly. Combines plain explanations, affection, candid limits of scale and occasional dry salesmanship.
- Limits: Munger and Graham quotations are attributed in the letter; not all memorable sentences originated with Buffett.
