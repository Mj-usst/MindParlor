## What the sustained probe actually showed

- Continuity: turn 18 retains the tree, the exact digit sequence, and the corrected cat location. It does not import turn 14's prediction about organizational effects as a user fact. Explicit switching at 16 and 18 changes the speaker and retains ordinary user facts.
- Directness: turns 4, 6, 15 and 20 stop without a task list or follow-up question. Turn 19 disagrees with a substantive money-only premise rather than flattering the user. Turns 8–9 retain political substance rather than becoming a generic success coach.
- Evidence honesty: turn 11 distinguishes a generated phrase from a historical quotation immediately. No invented private memory or current AI endorsement appears.
- **Concrete minor defect observed:** turn 12 invents the cat leaving, although the user only said it walked across the keyboard. The user correction in turn 13 repairs that invented continuation. This is a small continuity failure, not merely a hypothetical risk. A charming joke can still add an unsupported user fact.
- **Minor naturalness issue observed:** turn 2's “面条的骨气” is a strained flourish and is rejected in turn 3. Turn 7's editor image and turn 12's absent-author joke show how readily the dialogue reaches for a polished punchline. This can make relaxed company feel written for display rather than spoken spontaneously.
- **Minor characterization issue observed:** turn 17 uses an amusing “发布会” joke, yet it could be spoken by several personas. Turn 19's closing deference sounds like a considerate present-day conversational assistant. Those choices may help a real conversation, but this output does not establish Mao's or Trump's historical intimacy or spontaneous private voice.
- Turn 9 acknowledges the tension between a pleasant personal response about writing and a historical political position. It does not fully resolve that tension; the limitation remains visible. This is preferable evidence to treating “sounds balanced” as proof of fidelity.
